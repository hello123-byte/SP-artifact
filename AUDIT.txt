Manuscript-to-experiment audit
=============================
Scope: the 16-page SP555 PDF supplied in the workspace; Tables 1--6,
Figures 3--8, experimental settings and corresponding archived results.
No manuscript source was supplied. The original PDF and original experiments
were not edited. Suggested corrections below are ready to apply to the source.

CONFIRMED DISCREPANCIES

1. Table 1 and Section 5.2: AG6 host count.
   Manuscript: 617 hosts, and a range of 8--617 hosts.
   Actual: 676 hosts including the attack origin; layer sizes [1,75,300,300].
   Evidence: data/D11026/network_topology.json (676 devices), summary.json and
   tables/table1_recomputed.csv. All other Table 1 numeric statistics match.
   Correction: replace 617 by 676 in Table 1 and the surrounding host range.

2. Section 5.2.5 (RQ7) and Figure 5: budget condition.
   Manuscript says AG1--AG5 have no defense budget constraint.
   Figure 5(a--e) and quoted values are the B=300 records:
     AG1 Random: 0.939; AG2 Random: 0.782;
     AG3 GA: 0.6326666667; AG3 Random: 0.4666666667;
     AG4 GA: 0.
   Actual unbudgeted records differ:
     AG1 Random: 0.989; AG2 Random: 0.950;
     AG3 GA: 0.949; AG3 Random: 0.488;
     AG4 GA: 0.8006666667.
   Figure 5(f) shows B=300 for all methods and adds an UNBUDGETED curve
   only for Proposed, which reaches 0.614. Unbudgeted baselines for the
   replacement AG6 were not measured in this anytime batch.
   Recommended correction preserving the current figure and its numbers:
   "A defense budget of 300 is used for all six instances. For AG6, we
   additionally show the proposed method without a budget constraint. Each
   curve is the mean best-so-far benefit over seeds 7, 8 and 9 within 100 s."
   Replace the later sentence beginning "Under the unbudgeted setting..."
   with a statement confined to the added AG6 curve. Do not describe the
   budgeted baselines as measured unbudgeted baselines.
   The horizontal axis is symlog with a linear region below 0.02 s; describe
   it as such rather than a purely logarithmic axis.
   Alternative: replace all five panels and rewrite the numeric discussion
   using the explicitly supplied unbudgeted supplementary results.

3. Figure 7 and Table 6: case-study graph identity.
   Manuscript: 39 nodes, including separate vulProperty facts and
   managementAccess/managementService facts; named rules and target IDs 4/6.
   Actual solver input: 45 nodes, 45 edges, 12 OR, 13 AND, 20 LEAF;
   includes netAccess and zero-cost access rules, uses five-argument vulExists,
   and targets are IDs 1 and 32. This is more than a node-ID renaming.
   Evidence: SP/08_case_study/graph/{VERTICES,ARCS}.CSV;
   tables/case_actual_nodes.csv; figures/case_actual_graph.dot.
   Correction options: replace Figure 7/Table 6 with the actual graph/node
   list, or explicitly identify the 39-node illustration as a schematic
   abstraction and supply its reduction/mapping to the 45-node solver graph.
   Do not claim the 39-node appendix lists every node of the raw solver input.
   No claim of formal equivalence of the schematic is made by this audit.

MATCHING RESULTS

- Table 1: graph sizes, edges, node-type counts, target counts and hardenable
  vulnerabilities match all six inputs. AG1--AG5 host counts also match.
- Table 2: all 60 benefit/status cells agree with archived raw runs, including
  rounding, Random's 50-sample means, GA's 10-run means, and TL/EL entries.
- Table 3: all 12 rows agree with three-run build/solve/total means. All 36
  selected Proposed runs report optimal status and zero gap. Total is
  total_solve_game, not process wall time; it need not equal Build+Solve.
- Figure 3: cost replicate 1, base budget 300. Attack utility, cost-scale,
  budget and loss trends agree. Budget grid first improves at 60; this does
  not prove that 60 is the exact threshold (the base optimal defense costs 51).
- Figure 4: all 16 budget points agree with raw runs and summary means;
  GA at B=300 is approximately 0.263, Random -0.300, Proposed 0.949.
  Path-enumeration timeouts are missing benefit values, not zero benefits.
- Tables 4/5 and the S2 behavior in Figure 8 agree with the four configured
  scenarios. All 416 saved case-study solves match complete enumeration of
  the 32 patch subsets per scenario. S4 utilities are -66 and -14.
- The six large graphs satisfy the DAG and at-most-one-OR-prerequisite
  conditions used by the paper's cost-recursion correctness statement.

REPORTING QUALIFICATIONS

- The graph-generation paragraph should disclose the local directional-layered
  extension used for AG6. Frozen graph inputs are available here; the package
  does not claim to regenerate the networks with unmodified upstream defaults.
- PySCIPOpt 6.2.1 is recorded; the current installation reports SCIP 10.0.2,
  matching the manuscript, but the historical SCIP version was not separately
  archived. The archived
  environment does not independently establish the historical processor model
  or RAM. The manuscript's AMD 9955WX / approximately 192 GiB claim remains
  unverified against contemporaneous hardware records.
- Repeated runs do not redraw attack/defense cost maps. No statistical tests
  or generalization beyond these generated graphs are asserted by this audit.
- Runtimes are historical measurements, not newly rerun timings. Some original
  independent graph/baseline queues ran concurrently; the new portable runner
  is sequential and its wall times should not be substituted into old tables.
